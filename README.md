# 🧠 NeuroType — The Mindful Typing Metronome

## 📖 Overview | 概要

Welcome to **NeuroType**, a revolutionary touch-typing trainer that transforms the mundane act of keyboard practice into a **cognitive symphony**. While the original HapticTouchTypingTrainer focused on the physical haptic feedback loop, NeuroType elevates the experience by introducing **biometric-inspired pacing, adaptive neural pathways, and a gamified learning ecosystem**.

Imagine your keyboard not as a slab of plastic, but as a **piano for your thoughts**. NeuroType treats every keystroke as a note in a larger composition—your fingers are the musicians, and the software is the conductor ensuring perfect rhythm, harmony, and tempo. This is not just about typing faster; it's about typing *smarter*, *cleaner*, and with a state of flow that professional typists describe as "being in the zone."

By 2026, the average professional will spend over 40% of their workday typing. NeuroType prepares you for that reality by turning practice from a chore into a **meditative ritual**. ## ✨ Key Features | 主要機能

### 🎯 Adaptive Neural Pacing (ANP)
Unlike static drill exercises, NeuroType's **Adaptive Neural Pacing** engine observes your real-time typing metrics. It dynamically adjusts the difficulty gradient based on your *cognitive load*, not just your words-per-minute (WPM). If you are making consistent progress, the tempo increases subtly. If you show signs of fatigue or frustration (measured via error spikes), the system **gracefully decelerates** to consolidate muscle memory.

### 🧘 Flow State Induction
Our core philosophy is the **induction of a "Flow State"**—that magical place where time disappears and your fingers move as if possessed by a typing ghost. NeuroType uses a **binaural-inspired metronome** (visual and auditory) to guide your typing rhythm. The goal is not speed, but *effortless accuracy*. You will find yourself completing 20-minute sessions that feel like 5.

### 🌍 Multilingual Polyglot Drills
Supporting over **24 languages** including Japanese (日本語), Spanish, German, French, and Mandarin. The multilingual support goes beyond simple lorem-ipsum text. We utilize **corpus-specific frequency analysis** to ensure you practice the most common character combinations in your target language, making you fluent in *keyboard topography* faster.

### 📊 Cognitive Error Matrix (CEM)
Forget simple red X's on misspelled words. The **Cognitive Error Matrix** visualizes *why* you missed a key. Is it a lateral reach issue (index finger overreaching)? Is it a chord confusion (same-time press)? Or is it a rhythm break? The CEM uses a heat-map metaphor to pinpoint your physical keyboard weaknesses, offering specific drills to **iron out those neural wrinkles**.

### 🏆 Gamified "Zen Streak" System
NeuroType ditches aggressive leaderboards for a **"Zen Streak"** system. You don't lose points for errors; you lose *peace*. Your progress is measured by a calm, flowing river visual. Keep your streak alive to see the river grow into a massive, serene ocean. This fosters a growth mindset without the anxiety of competition.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack | 技術スタック

NeuroType is built on a robust, future-proof architecture designed for extensibility:

- **Frontend:** React 19 (with Concurrent Rendering for ultra-smooth animations) & TypeScript. The UI is a **responsive UI** that fluidly adapts from a 4K monitor to a mobile phone screen using CSS Grid and Flexbox.
- **Backend (Optional Sync):** Rust-based WebAssembly for the core calculation engine, allowing for real-time keystroke analysis without a server round-trip.
- **Data Storage:** Local-first SQLite for privacy, with optional cloud sync via IndexedDB for cross-device progress tracking.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA compliant. Includes **dyslexia-friendly font options** and a high-contrast "Night Sand" theme.
